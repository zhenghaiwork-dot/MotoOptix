# Product Publishing Rules

These rules protect MotoOptix from publishing a product that does not exist as described.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. One public product record maps to one selected supplier configuration and revision.
2. Do not combine the best display, camera, IP rating and certification claims from different suppliers.
3. Separate native sensor resolution from encoded file resolution.
4. Identify which component an ingress-protection claim covers.
5. Certifications and reports must be matched by model, applicant, revision and relevant radio module.
6. Use “supplier-declared” when a claim has not been independently measured.
7. Use “available option” only when the option is quoted for the same platform.
8. Do not publish an MOQ or lead time without a dated commercial confirmation.
9. Do not claim manufacturer status, factory ownership, client cases or test capability without evidence.
10. Archive the approved source files and date of every published change.

## Required record before a fixed product page

- Internal MotoOptix SKU
- Supplier company and model
- Hardware and firmware revision
- Sample identifier and receipt date
- Evidence stage
- Approved specification source
- Available documents and their coverage
- Standard and optional components
- MOQ, sample price, lead time and validity date
- Warranty, DOA and spare-unit terms
- Approved product photos and usage permission
- Page owner and last review date

Until this record exists, the public page must remain a clearly labeled reference platform without fixed performance or compliance claims.
